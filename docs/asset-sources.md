# Event content and artwork

Event date, timing, category fees, benefits and policy baseline were supplied by the organiser for reuse from [the central event website](https://www.sekhonmarathon.com/). Suratgarh, Desert Braves and the restricted audience are organiser-provided changes. Route maps and real QR payment details remain unconfigured. The organiser supplied contact numbers 8838463776 and 7027964880.

The shared Sekhon event logo is from [the reference site](https://www.sekhonmarathon.com/). The Gwalior wing crest, Gwalior-labelled artwork and payment QR are not used.

The Rajasthan background is original AI-generated canvas-style artwork. It is an illustration, not an event photograph or a depiction of the actual Suratgarh route. It was inspired by:

- [PIB, Sekhon IAF Marathon, 2 November 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2185553&lang=2&reg=48)
- [HAL Jaguar reference](https://www.hal-india.co.in/product/jaguar)
- [PIB Jaguar photograph](https://www.pib.gov.in/ShowAlbum.aspx?albumid=160916)
- [Ministry of Defence Mi-17V5 induction](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=80383)
- [IAF Mi-35 photograph mirrored on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MI-35.JPG)

The final image is served as a 315 KB WebP to reduce mobile page weight.

## Station community redesign, 5 September 2026

**Geography correction:** the organiser clarified that the canal is in the surrounding region, outside the station, visible from aircraft. The active artwork is now `suratgarh-station-desktop-v2.webp` (1774×887, 284982 bytes) and `suratgarh-station-mobile-v2.webp` (1200×900, 243804 bytes). The built-in image-generation tool edited the earlier paintings to put runners on landscaped station roads, with residences, lawns and trees. The canal is a thin distant ribbon beyond the community. The page caption explicitly explains the distinction. Both compressed images were visually inspected; Sharp performed format conversion and resizing only. The previous files described below remain historical versions, not the displayed hero. These paintings are artistic impressions, not photographs or accurate station layouts.

The user requested a thriving, green desert city/station rather than barren dunes, and greater prominence for Air Force Station Suratgarh. The current original canvas images are `suratgarh-canal-desktop.webp` (1774×887, 321574 bytes) and `suratgarh-canal-mobile.webp` (1200×900, 259544 bytes). Both were generated with the built-in image tool and inspected at their natural 2:1 / 4:3 ratios. They depict an artistic impression of the Indira canal, tree line, maintained community landscape and running promenade, with IAF Jaguar, Mi-17 V5 and Mi-35 aircraft. They are not photographs, approved route maps or a promise of a flypast. The Jaguar form was corrected using a visual reference from [Rolls-Royce's Adour product page](https://www.rolls-royce.com/products-and-services/defence/aerospace/combat-jets/adour.aspx); that reference photograph is not published as site content.

`nirmal-jit-singh-sekhon-portrait.webp` is the 200×150 original archival portrait from the [Ministry of Defence Gallantry Awards profile](https://gallantryawards.gov.in/awardee/3432). It is displayed small, without invented detail. The [portal website policy, section V](https://gallantryawards.gov.in/assets/pdf/Website_Policy.pdf), permits accurate reproduction with acknowledgement, excluding identified third-party/user-submitted content. This is the main official profile portrait. The biography and short quotation are based on the profile's official citation; the words are attributed to the citation, not presented as something Sekhon said. IAF-only PVC status is supported by [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1754825&lang=2&reg=48).

`ap-singh-sekhon-2025.webp` is a genuine photograph from the [2 November 2025 Ministry of Defence/PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2185553&lang=2&reg=48). The source confirms Air Chief Marshal AP Singh participated in the 21 KM run. The visible caption states Delhi and 2025. [Original photograph](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/userfiles/image/WhatsAppImage2025-11-02at5.12.20PM(2)VZVG.jpeg). It is 1500×1600, 139226 bytes, encoded as WebP without retouching. This does not announce his attendance at Suratgarh in 2026.

The running-benefit copy is restrained and sourced from [WHO physical activity guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity) and [CDC benefits of physical activity](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/benefits/index.html). It describes regular physical activity, not guaranteed outcomes of one race.

The `sekhoniafmarathon` Instagram profile was requested as an idea source. Direct access failed and no specific post was verified. No unverified Instagram photograph, quote or announcement is reproduced. Event imagery is grounded in official sources above.

Public Sans and Barlow Condensed are self-hosted from Google Fonts; their OFL licences are distributed in `public/fonts`. The DB app icon is a purpose-made sun/canal monogram, not an official military crest. Original event-message copy is not attributed to Sekhon, AP Singh or another real person.

### Text-free home artwork

Using the organiser-supplied Legacy of Courage poster as the edit target, the built-in image tool produced desktop and mobile source images that were delivery-compressed without changing their composition into `suratgarh-home-hero-desktop-v3.jpg` (1774 × 887) and `suratgarh-home-hero-mobile-v3.jpg` (1448 × 1086). The homepage uses them as a text-free campaign illustration: the live HTML overlay supplies only “Desert Braves”, “Air Force Station Suratgarh” and “The Land of Sun and Sand”. The artwork is an artistic impression of the station community, runners and aircraft, not a route map, a flypast announcement or a documentary photograph.

The gallery photos were resized proportionally and re-encoded as WebP, without retouching or invented details. Small separate thumbnails support a compact gallery; original source metadata is excluded from the public derivatives. Their use is based on the organiser's supplied materials and instruction, not an independently researched public reuse licence.

The long visible canvas caption was removed at the organiser's request during the compact redesign. Its alternative text still identifies the illustration as a canvas painting with the canal in the distant surrounding region.
