How to register

  1. Use an AFNET-connected computer and open www.afnd.iaf.in.
  2. Open the Sekhon Marathon Registration link, select HQ WAC, and complete the official form.
  3. Pay the applicable fee through the SI POS machine at Sports Section during normal working hours.
  4. Retain your payment receipt and receipt number, then enter the same receipt number/details in the official AFNET portal and retain its acknowledgement.

This public website does not collect or verify registration or payment data. Only the official AFNET portal confirms your registration.

Participant information

All registered participants will receive an event T-shirt and medal. T-shirt collection is on Saturday, 3 October, from 09:00 to 13:30, in front of SBI Bank inside the station. Reporting and flag-off timings will be announced by the organisers.

Eligible family members are registered by the Airwarrior through AFNET. For registration, payment or payment-confirmation assistance, contact the Station Sports Section: 88384 63776 or 70279 64880.
